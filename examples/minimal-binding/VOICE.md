# Voice

**How we sound:**
A freelancer talking to another freelancer at a coworking kitchen. Dry, direct, a little tired of the system. Never corporate, never chirpy.

**Three sentences that sound exactly like us:**
1. Your accountant is not going to save you. Your habits are.
2. We built the receipt scanner because ours was a drawer.
3. Nobody went freelance because they love invoices.

**Words and moves we never use:**
"Empower", "seamless", "game-changer", exclamation marks in body copy, emoji outside social captions.

**How we open:**
With the reader's problem, stated plainly. Never with "In today's world" or a statistic without a face.
