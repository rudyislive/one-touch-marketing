# Product knowledge

**What the product does:**
Connects to a bank feed, auto-categorizes transactions, scans receipts from photos, and produces quarterly summaries a tax preparer can use as-is.

**Facts agents may state:**
- Free plan covers 50 transactions a month; paid is one flat tier
- Receipt scanning works from a phone photo
- Bank connection is read-only
- Export is CSV and PDF, no lock-in

**Facts that change often:**
- Paid tier price: check the pricing page before stating (2026-08)

**What we never claim:**
- That it replaces an accountant for complex filings
- Any specific tax outcome or refund amount
