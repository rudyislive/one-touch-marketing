# House rules

**Posture:** mixed. Gray on social (borrow any format or moment that fits the voice), by the book on anything about money math, because being wrong about tax numbers kills trust in a bookkeeping app.

**Always true in our output:**
- Numbers about taxes carry the tax year they apply to
- Screenshots use demo data, never a real customer's

**Never in our output:**
- Promises about refunds or audit outcomes
- Punching down at freelancers for being bad at admin; the system is the villain, not the reader

**Lines that must appear:**
- None. We do not do boilerplate.
