# Identity

**Who we are:**
Ledgerline is a bookkeeping app for freelancers who hate bookkeeping. It turns a shoebox of receipts and a bank feed into clean quarterly numbers without an accountant.

**The one-line version:**
Bookkeeping for people who would rather be working.

**Site / domain:**
ledgerline.example

**The audience:**
Freelancers and one-person businesses, mostly design and dev, who do their books at 11pm the night before a tax deadline. They stop scrolling for tax horror stories, money confessions, and anything that makes admin feel less lonely.
